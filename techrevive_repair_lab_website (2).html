Welcome to TechRevive Repair Lab

At TechRevive Repair Lab, we bring damaged devices back to life. Our technicians repair smartphones, tablets, laptops, desktop computers, and gaming consoles using reliable tools, quality parts, and honest service.

Whether your device has a cracked screen, battery issue, water damage, charging problem, or software error, we provide fast diagnostics and professional repair solutions.

About

Company History

TechRevive Repair Lab was founded in 2021 to provide affordable and trustworthy electronics repair services for students, families, professionals, and local businesses in Riverside.

Ownership

The business operates as a partnership owned by two experienced electronics repair specialists who wanted to create a modern, customer-focused repair shop.

Services Offered

We repair phones, tablets, laptops, desktop computers, gaming consoles, screens, batteries, charging ports, cameras, and software systems.

Competition

We compete with major repair chains by offering faster service, lower pricing, personalized support, and direct communication with repair technicians.

Community Involvement

TechRevive supports electronic recycling and helps local students learn basic technology care, maintenance, and repair awareness.

Products or Services

Smartphone Repair

We repair cracked screens, broken cameras, charging ports, water damage, battery issues, speaker problems, and software malfunctions for iPhone and Android devices.

Computer Repair

Our technicians diagnose and repair laptops and desktops with hardware failure, slow performance, viruses, screen damage, storage issues, and operating system problems.

Gaming Console Repair

We repair PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo systems experiencing overheating, HDMI failures, fan issues, disk errors, controller problems, and software issues.

Employment

Electronics Repair Technician

Responsibilities: Diagnose device issues, replace damaged parts, test repairs, organize workstations, and assist customers with repair questions.

Requirements: High school diploma, repair experience, problem-solving skills, and professional communication.

Salary/Benefits: $22/hour, paid training, employee discounts, flexible scheduling.

Customer Service Representative

Responsibilities: Greet customers, schedule repairs, answer calls, process payments, and explain pricing.

Requirements: Customer service experience, basic computer skills, organization, and friendly attitude.

Salary/Benefits: $18/hour, flexible schedule, discounts, advancement opportunities.

Store Manager

Responsibilities: Supervise employees, manage inventory, oversee operations, handle customer concerns, and track sales.

Requirements: Management experience, leadership skills, electronics knowledge, and strong communication.

Salary/Benefits: $55,000/year, health insurance, bonuses, paid vacation.
